# Code of Conduct

Somna welcomes people with different backgrounds, experience levels, identities, and perspectives. We want project discussions and contributions to be respectful, constructive, and focused on the work.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and considerate.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome questions and explain decisions in good faith.
- Respect privacy. Do not share another person's health information, personal details, or private messages without their consent.
- Accept that people may have different needs, experiences, and points of view.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, and repeated disruptive conduct are not acceptable. Publishing someone else's private or health information is not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainer privately through GitHub. If you cannot reach the maintainer or believe the behavior violates GitHub's rules, use [GitHub's reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-community-guidelines#reporting-abuse-or-spam).

The maintainer will review reports fairly and take action appropriate to the situation, including a warning, removal of content, or exclusion from project spaces. Reports will be handled as privately as possible.

This code of conduct applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where someone is representing Somna.
